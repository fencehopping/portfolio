A poker passion project.

I started this idea years ago after becoming obsessed with the iPhone game Letterpress. Letterpress is a turn based word game that instantly pairs you other players for quick competitive match ups. I wanted to take that vibe and apply it to heads up poker, and then I discovered Wordle. I love the idea of taking the idea further, by having a daily heads up poker tournament, where you're allotted a certain amount of chips daily, and you get ranked at the end of the day based on how high you can stack your chips by pairing with other players.

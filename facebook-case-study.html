Case Study

Bridging AI Mapping w/ the OpenStreetMaps community

Role: Lead Product Designer
‍Duration: 1 year
‍Tools Used: Sketch, Invision
‍Team: Backend engineering (5), Front-end Engineering (2), Product (1)
The Problem to Solve

Goodbye, google API.

In 2017, Facebook removed all Google powered mapping related API's from the app. In order to replace location based data in the product, they integrated with the open source platform OpenStreetMaps. Utilizing AI modeling, Facebook was able to upload terabytes of map feature data to OpenStreetMaps. Due to the nature of the OpenStreetMaps community, this caused a rift and Facebook soon found it's account access blocked. We needed a way to empower the OpenStreetMaps community to utilize Facebook's mapping tools.

Goals:

• Integrate Facebook's AI detected mapping features with OSM tools.
• Increase the speed of mapping the globe significantly.
• Decrease the inaccuracy of feature data added to the global map.
Research

Methods:

• Data modeling breakdown with the Engineering team.
• Attending Mapathon's with OpenStreetMaps community members.
• Beta product user feedback.

Mapathons:

I attended ~10 different mapathons to understand how users interacted with the current mapping tool. I also got to see their pain points first hand, and better understand their workflows. This research was crucial in going back to the drawing board and starting to integrate Facebook's RapiD data into the mapping tool.
Ideation & Design

The original plan controller shown below, was quite confusing all around, from it's hierarchy and layout, to button stylings, to the information overload and unintuitive buttons.

For initial iterations, I explored a cleaner UI and layout, while directing the user to inputs that were required to run their desired plan type.

Pushing the design even further to simplify and clean up the UI, I started to create a unique interface to match the action of the user, and align with the needs of the persona. Results & Impact

Metrics:

• Reduced lead time to sign by 4 months on 6 new SMB's.
• New smb clients required 80% less sales engineering hours to be self-driven and productive with Arrow.

Qualitative Feedback:

• Watching customer session replays, I got to witness daily use of tooltips, tutorials, and new onboarding material in order for clients to be proactive about unclocking themselves and learning how to use the new features/product.

Qualitative Feedback:

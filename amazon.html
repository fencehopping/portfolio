Amazon's 2025 Dash Cart Redesign

Dash Cart is an interactive shopping companion, allowing you to scan, weigh, and bag items directly in the cart, the use a Dash Cart lane for a seamless, contactless payment experience.

New Cart.

The task at hand: to redesign a user experience that would fit in both portrait mode and landscape mode, as well as integrate new technology and functionality like maps, shopping lists, coupons, and a scale on the cart handlebars.

Vast offers decision-making tools that help and support customers in purchasing homes, cars, and vacations. I designed and shipped several iPad apps, including Southwest’s flight/hotel search app, and two proprietary SaaS products for Car Dealerships and Real Estate Organizations.
Southwest Airlines
Vast powered Southwest Airline's flight and hotel search, and we were hired to design and build their iPad app. I was the lead and sole designer for this project.
Car Story
Car Story was an iPad app developed to assist sales teams at automobile dealerships. It provided a browsable lot inventory, CRM management, and robust inventory details and analytics for both the sales team and the customer.

I was the lead and sole designer.
Home Story
Home Story was an iPad app developed to assist real estate agents out in the field. It provided powerful search, data, and demographic data related to available real estate that an agent could use when interfacing with a potential buyer.

I was the lead and sole designer.

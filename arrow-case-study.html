Case Study

Full UI / UX Redesign

Role: Lead Product Designer
‍Duration: 8 months
‍Tools Used: Figma
‍Team: Backend engineering (2), Front-end Engineering (3), Product (1)
The Problem to Solve

A powerful product, with a confusing UX.

The initial version of Arrow was designed by a small team of engineers over the course of 5 years. It is a single page app that became overcrowded with features and functionality, with little to no thought put towards the user journey or experience. I joined during a code re-write from Angular to React, allowing me the opportunity to tackle a redesign in tandem with development.

Goals:

• Decrease onboarding time.
• Define personas and create workflows accordingly.
• Redesign layout to create a unified user journey.
Research

Methods:

• Weekly client interviews and job shadowing.
• Weekly client-facing feature ideation design reviews.
• Weekly sales call sit-ins.

Findings:

I was able to funnel groups of users into 4 specific personas:
• Engineer - focused on network build-outs, equipment, and fiber lengths.
• Analyst - focused on competition analysis, and audits.
• Admin - power user, associated with sales engineering and internal use.
• Planner - focused on winning government grants and maximizing ROI.
Ideation & Design

The original plan controller shown below, was quite confusing all around, from it's hierarchy and layout, to button stylings, to the information overload and unintuitive buttons.

For initial iterations, I explored a cleaner UI and layout, while directing the user to inputs that were required to run their desired plan type.

Pushing the design even further to simplify and clean up the UI, I started to create a unique interface to match the action of the user, and align with the needs of the persona. A user can view (an previously run plan), they can run a new plan, or edit an existing plan. Each action has it's own functionality that was previously all crammed into the same small container.

Results & Impact

Metrics:

• Reduced lead time to sign by 4 months on 6 new SMB's.
• New smb clients required 80% less sales engineering hours to be self-driven and productive with Arrow.

Qualitative Feedback:

• Watching customer session replays, I got to witness daily use of tooltips, tutorials, and new onboarding material in order for clients to be proactive about unclocking themselves and learning how to use the new features/product.

Qualitative Feedback:

• Onboarded 6 new SMB's from Q2 2022-Q1 2023
• Added a total of $7.5mm in recurring revenue, a 48% in record total.
